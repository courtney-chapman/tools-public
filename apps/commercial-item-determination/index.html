2. Choose the definition

For a deviation or another rule, add the reason it applies below.

The app cannot evaluate an unlisted rule's legal criteria. Enter the criteria and your evidence analysis for each item or service below.

Help with terms in the questions
Substantial quantities
Use sales evidence to show how much of the exact item is sold commercially. A listing alone does not show quantity.
Customary nongovernmental use
People or organizations outside government use it for their own purposes.
Minor modification
Assess the Government-specific change under the selected definition, including its effect on function, essential characteristics, and purpose.
COTS
A narrower product category: product (1), substantial commercial sales, same form, no modification, and no bulk cargo.

4. Items and services
