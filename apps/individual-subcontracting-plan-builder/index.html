Individual Subcontracting Plan Builder

Prepare goals and worksheets for an individual subcontracting plan.

FAR 19.704 · FAR 52.219-9

Work through one tab at a time, review the checks, then preview and export. The tool organizes information and checks calculations; it does not determine clause applicability, certify compliance, submit reports, or approve a plan.

2. Subcontracting opportunities

List the principal supplies and services. Category tags identify possible recipients; they do not allocate the full amount to every selected category.

3. Contract values by period

Enter the estimated total contract value for the base period and each option. The combined value and each period’s share calculate automatically. These values are the denominator for goals expressed as a percent of contract dollars.

4. Goals by period

Direct dollars are projected subcontracts charged to this contract. If you include shared indirect costs, enter only the portion allocated to this contract in the separate table. Enter dollars once; the two versions use different percentage bases. Blank means unknown; enter 0 only for an actual zero goal. Amounts may include commas or a $ sign.

6. Compliance assurances

Review each generated commitment against the governing clause and solicitation. Edits remain yours. Clearing a required assurance leaves the plan incomplete.

Check the plan

Open a topic below to see its findings. Checks identify missing entries, calculation conflicts, and items for human review; they do not certify FAR compliance.

Preview and export

Choose a working plan or a submission plan with both percentage bases. Both use the same entered goal dollars. If the Contracting Officer requires contract-dollar goals, use the submission plan. Drafts may contain [INSERT] prompts. Print saves only the selected output.

The AI handoff copies information only when you press the button. Check your organization’s authorization before using an external AI service.
