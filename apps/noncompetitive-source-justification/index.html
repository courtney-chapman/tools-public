3Justification Option
This is a practical description of your rationale, not an automatic regulatory exception.
Where relevant, include documented dates, quantities, lead times, transition effort, or cost impacts. Leave out numbers you cannot support.
References are copied into the justification. The tool does not inspect files or verify evidence.
