# Scope and limitations

This standalone tool is a demonstration, not a property management system or a compliance determination.

- Inventory methods, frequency, coverage, final-inventory requirements, reporting, and approvals depend on the actual contract and the contractor's procedures.
- A selected subset is a working scope, not proof of a valid sampling method or full inventory.
- Imported property data is a working copy. The tool does not change source files, post adjustments, or establish an authoritative record.
- A variance or identification/location discrepancy needs investigation. Shortages need prompt handling under applicable loss and reporting procedures; a count difference alone is not a final loss determination.
- Unexpected property is an observation for review, not an ownership or accountability determination. An item absent from the property records needs review under applicable record-establishment procedures.
- The app does not identify sensitive property or apply any special escalation or notification rule. Users must follow their procedures for sensitive items.
- The app does not provide a complete final-inventory or contract-closeout package. Additional coverage, condition, future-need, subcontractor, approval, and reporting work may apply.
- Printing or exporting a report does not submit inventory results to the Government or certify the inventory.
- All working data stays in browser memory and is lost on reload or close. The tool has no backend, persistent storage, telemetry, or automatic regulatory updates.
- Source versions are fixed at Revision 1. No maintenance or support commitment is made.
