# Public evidence catalog — Revision 1

**Status:** Revision 1 prompt catalog for the offline application.
**Source check:** 2026-11-15. See [regulatory basis](regulatory-basis.md) for versions and limits.

This catalog uses the 22 element names and numbers in the [DCMA Job Aid, Rev. 4][J]. Each element has two broad preparation prompts. **DCMA assessment guidance** means the Job Aid discusses that evidence category; it does not make every example a universal contractor requirement. **Regulatory requirement** and **Conditional requirement** labels describe the underlying clause obligation only when it applies to the user's contract; they do not require use of this toolkit or its suggested reference format. No prompt asks the app to decide applicability or evidence sufficiency.

## Catalog behavior in the app

- All 44 prompts begin **Not Reviewed**. A user's **Not Applicable** choice and explanation are stored as preparation notes only.
- An evidence reference may describe one document, a record-set ZIP, or a backup and may be linked to several elements. It is not linked to individual prompts and does not establish that every prompt in an element is supported. Duplicate titles do not automatically merge references.
- Counts and outstanding lists represent user-entered statuses only. They are not compliance scores, findings, or evidence-sufficiency determinations.
- The catalog contains no clause-version decision engine and no instructions for DCMA sampling or control testing.
