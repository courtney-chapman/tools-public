# Limits and user responsibilities

This toolkit is an evidence-reference and working-item organizer. It is not an official PMSA report, a compliance assessment, a contract-clause determination, or a substitute for professional judgment.

- An element marked outside scope or a prompt marked Not Applicable reflects the user's preparation note only. Check the actual contract and Government direction.
- Available means the user recorded a status; the app does not inspect documents or determine whether evidence is complete, accurate, or sufficient.
- Counts and outstanding lists are organizational. Question, finding, and risk entries are supplied by the user; the app does not create official findings, assign risk, or verify responses.
- Dashboard evidence colors summarize the two user-entered prompt statuses for each element. Separate finding colors summarize user-entered finding stage, risk rating, and open/closed status. Green does not mean compliant, and red does not establish a deficiency. CAGE codes and contract numbers are user-entered scope labels; the app does not verify them or determine which clauses apply.
- The app does not make property-record updates, investigate losses, authorize disposition, or submit Government reports.
- Evidence references are entered as text. A ZIP or backup can be described as one reference, but no files are uploaded, opened, counted, or extracted. The app does not verify that a referenced file exists or that it supports any prompt.
- Provided-by, version, and request/received dates reflect the user's description; the app does not authenticate origin, compare revisions, verify dates, or establish a chain of custody. Optional sampling fields belong to each of the 22 elements and record a described population, selection method, size, performer, timing, steps, and limitations. The app does not select records, perform sampling, or judge adequacy.
- Gather and Documents CSV imports accept user-entered text only. Passing import validation confirms the file's structure and allowed values; it does not verify the evidence, sampling method, document existence, or compliance. The Gather CSV updates listed rows; the Documents CSV replaces the reference list after confirmation. Session JSON is the complete working-session backup.
- Session data stays in page memory. Refreshing or closing the page loses unsaved entries. Exported JSON, CSV, and PDF files may contain sensitive references, questions, responses, and finding notes and should be handled in an authorized environment.
- Offline operation alone does not make a device or environment suitable for classified information, CUI, export-controlled information, or other restricted information.
- Public source versions were checked for Revision 1; the app does not update them automatically.

The 22 published DCMA elements help organize evidence preparation. The applicable FAR and DFARS clauses, contract terms, deviations, and Government instructions determine obligations.
