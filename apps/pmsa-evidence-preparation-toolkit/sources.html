How to read the prompts

FAR 52.245-1 describes 10 property-management outcomes. The DCMA Job Aid divides its review into 22 topics. This tool uses those topics to help you organize document references.

The Job Aid gives examples of records an administrator may review. An example in the Job Aid is not automatically a document every contractor must have. Your contract, incorporated clauses, deviations, and Government direction determine what applies.
