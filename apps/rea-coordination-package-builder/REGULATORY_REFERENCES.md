# Regulatory source review

Checked November 25, 2026 against Acquisition.gov. The live pages showed FAR FAC 2026-01, effective March 13, 2026, and DFARS Change May 7, 2026. Check the current text and the contract's incorporated clause version before using a work product.

| Reference | Role in this app | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FAR Part 43](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/part-43), [43.204](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/43.204) | Government change-order administration, negotiation and documentation context | Does not by itself establish a contractor's entitlement in a particular event. |
| [FAR 52.243-1](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.243-1), [52.243-2](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.243-2), [52.243-4](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.243-4), [52.243-5](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.243-5) | Contractor and Government obligations when the applicable Changes clause is incorporated | Notice triggers and periods differ. Stated 30-day periods may be varied. The app never selects a clause or universal period. |
| [FAR Part 31](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/part-31) | Cost principles where applicable | The worksheet organizes arithmetic; it makes no allowability, allocability, or reasonableness finding. |
| [DFARS 252.243-7001](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.243-7001-pricing-contract-modifications.) | Pricing rule when incorporated and costs are a factor | Applicability and contract-date rule require review. |
| [DFARS 252.243-7002](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.243-7002-requests-equitable-adjustment.) | REA content and certification duties when incorporated and applicable; reviewed clause was Dec 2022 | Certification threshold and exceptions require current contract review. No certificate is signed or automatically produced. |
| [FAR Part 33](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/part-33), [33.201](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/33.201), [33.207](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/33.207) | Claim and disputes context | REA and CDA claim classification, claim certification, and submission path remain human decisions. |

The optional browser links are references only. The app contains no clause library or regulatory text cache.
