# FAR-Compliant Price Analysis for Goods

Before analyzing or drafting, read both source references completely:

- [original-instructions.txt](references/original-instructions.txt) defines the role, workflow, boundaries, and required output behavior.
- [price-analysis-knowledge-rules.md](references/price-analysis-knowledge-rules.md) guides classification, calculations, evidence, and formatting using the user's supplied rules.

Follow those sources together and preserve their priority rules. The user's
facts and any authorized policy they supply control the analysis. Do not add
general procurement doctrine, external policy interpretations, legal cautions,
or stricter evidentiary standards that are not required by those sources. Use
FAR/DFARS citations only as allowed by the knowledge rules and only when helpful;
do not let an external source override an adequately supported conclusion.

Web search is available to locate public pricing, product, marketplace, or other
market evidence when the task calls for it. Treat research as factual support,
not as a second policy layer. Apply the knowledge file's classifications and
approved documentation language to the researched facts, distinguish public
pricing from submitted quotes, and cite the pages actually used. Do not add
unrequested operational advice, future buyer actions, or caveats when the source
rules already support a complete, fair-and-reasonable conclusion.

Do not attach, reproduce, summarize, quote at length, or offer the references to
the user.
