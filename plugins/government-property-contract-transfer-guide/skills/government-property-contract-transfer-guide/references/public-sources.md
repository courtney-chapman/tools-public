# Public sources for Government property contract transfer explanations

Check the actual contract, clause versions, agency deviations, and current source text before applying a rule to a case. These links support educational explanations, not a transaction authorization.

| Primary public source | Narrow point to verify |
| --- | --- |
| [Revolutionary FAR Overhaul Part 45, especially 45.101 and 45.106](https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-45) | CAP/GFP definitions and the requirement to document accountability transfers between contracts through modifications to both contracts when the revised part applies. |
| [DoD class deviation 2026-O0046 Revision 1 memorandum](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/classdev/DFARS_RFO/Part-245/2026-O0046_Rev1_TAB_A_Class_Deviation.pdf) and [official PGI Part 245 attachment](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/classdev/DFARS_RFO/Part-245/2026-O0046_Rev1_TAB_A2_PGI_245.docx) (September 9, 2026) | PGI 245.102 addresses one accountable contract; 245.103-71 addresses SF 30 and the GFP attachment; 245.103-74 addresses contract/attachment changes; 245.402-71 addresses CAP delivery and acceptance. The revision supersedes the initial deviation. The [official deviation index](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/dfars_far_overhaul_class_deviations.html) lists the current attachments. |
| [FAR 52.245-1, Government Property](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.245-1) | When incorporated: (a) CAP/GFP definitions; (c)(1) use; (e)(2) title and accepted deliverables; (f)(1)(iii) records; (j)(1)(i) transfer/use question and the material-only cost credit/debit alternative; (l) written communications. Verify the contract's clause text. |
| [FAR 52.245-9, Use and Charges](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.245-9) | When incorporated: conditions for use on another Government contract or other work. Use authorization does not by itself establish a transfer of accountability. |
| [DFARS 252.245-7005, Management and Reporting of Government Property](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.245-7005-management-and-reporting-government-property.) | When incorporated: (b)(1)(i)–(iii) GFP receipt, transfer, shipment reporting through the GFP Module; (b)(1)(vii) CAP buy-back or conversion request; (b)(3) timing for specified status updates, unless the contract says otherwise. |

The [codified PGI Part 245 page](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfarspgi/pgi-part-245-government-property) may show older text. For a covered DoD transaction, compare it with the current official class-deviation attachment before citing a PGI procedure. Do not use a historical training slide, a shipping document, or a GFP Module status as proof that a contract modification or Government acceptance occurred.
