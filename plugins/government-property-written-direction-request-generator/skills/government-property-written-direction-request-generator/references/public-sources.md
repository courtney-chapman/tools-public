# Public authority for direction requests

Check the actual contract or agreement, incorporated clause versions, applicable deviations, and designated communication route. These public sources support narrow drafting safeguards; they do not establish the facts or authority in a particular case.

| Source | Narrow relevance |
| --- | --- |
| [FAR 52.245-1, Government Property](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.245-1) | Paragraph (b)(2) addresses continuing stewardship; (c)(1) addresses use; (f)(1)(viii)(A) addresses authorized use, movement, and storage and excess reporting; (f)(1)(x) addresses closeout; (j) addresses contractor inventory disposal; (l) requires written communications under the clause. Apply only when incorporated. |
| [FAR 45.106, Transferring accountability](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/45.106) | Transfer between existing contracts differs from permission to use property on another contract. The published FAR provision calls for modifications to both gaining and losing contracts. Verify the governing contract and any applicable deviation. |
| [FAR 52.245-9, Use and Charges](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.245-9) | When incorporated, addresses authorized use on other Government work and conditional rental or non-Government use. Do not impose its rental or request details when inapplicable. |
| [FAR 1.602-1, Contracting officer authority](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/1.602-1) and [FAR 43.102, Contract modifications](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/43.102) | Contracting officers bind the Government within delegated authority; only authorized contracting officers execute modifications. A person's title or reported verbal statement alone does not establish the scope of authority for a particular action. |
| [DFARS 252.245-7005, Management and Reporting of Government Property](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/252.245-7005-management-and-reporting-government-property.) | When incorporated, adds DoD Government property reporting and disposition processes. It does not apply to every Government property matter. |

**Drafting boundary:** A request for clarification is correspondence, not approval, a transfer instrument, a disposition schedule, or a property record update. A pending request does not suspend separately applicable stewardship, reporting, closeout, or disposition obligations. Identify a case-specific question rather than deciding authorization from an incomplete record. Public text is not a substitute for the incorporated contract, authorized delegation, or written direction.
