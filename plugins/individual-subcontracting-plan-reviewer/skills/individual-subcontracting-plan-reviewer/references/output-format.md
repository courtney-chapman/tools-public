# Output guidance

Choose only the requested scope. A full **Individual Subcontracting Plan Review** uses:

1. Plan identification and applicability: plan type, contract/solicitation basis, clause date/alternate, contractor size, and unresolved questions.
2. Executive review summary: material strengths, conflicts, and limits, without a compliance certification.
3. Required plan element matrix: each applicable FAR 52.219-9(d)(1)–(15) item, supplement where relevant, evidence status, and correction. Group related paragraphs if still traceable; a heading alone is not completion.
4. Subcontracting goal validation: entered versus calculated dollars/percentages, denominator, overlap treatment, and math gaps.
5. Base and option reconciliation: each actual period and contract-wide dollar-weighted summary.
6. Supplies and services opportunities: projected work, supporting assumptions, and category attribution limits.
7. Goal methodology and evidence: historical data, forecast purchasing, supplier research, source-identification method, and indirect allocation.
8. Assurances and reporting: prospective plan commitments separated from post-award ISR/SSR duties; clause/alternate caveats.
9. Findings and missing information: table with **Complete**, **Incomplete**, **Inconsistent**, **Unsupported**, or **Requires Review**; exact element/authority, evidence or gap, significance, and correction.
10. Recommended corrections and next steps: only actions needed to resolve material findings, preserving user ownership of the plan.

A goals-only request gets a compact calculation table and material flags, without a forced full-plan checklist. A methodology-only request evaluates the stated basis and evidence. For revision, provide a copy-ready revised passage or plan and a concise change log; do not silently change commitments or numbers.

For a requested **complete draft plan**, use a professional heading and clearly label **Draft — not Government approved**. Include contract and period identification; six category goal dollars and both percentage bases if required; principal subcontracted supplies/services; goal and source-identification methods; indirect-cost treatment; administrator and duties; equitable-opportunity measures; applicable downstream, cooperation, reporting, records, proposal-use, explanation, communication, and payment assurances; and identified supplement-specific terms. Preserve all user supplied commitments and values. Use targeted `[INSERT: specific missing information]` placeholders for essential gaps. Do not invent signatures or approval. Put review commentary and the short validation reminder outside the copy-ready plan.
