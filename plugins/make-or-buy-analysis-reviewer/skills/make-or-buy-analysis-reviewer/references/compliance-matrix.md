# Public regulatory compliance matrix

Verified against the official Acquisition.gov codified FAR (FAC 2026-01, effective 2026-03-13), DFARS (change 2026-05-07), and official FAR-overhaul/deviation pages on 2026-11-20. This matrix is a conditional review aid. Check the governing solicitation or contract, agency deviation, clause text and date, alternates, and current official sources for each case. A regulatory provision addressed to a contracting officer does not, by itself, impose a contractor submission duty. “Mandatory” below means mandatory once the stated trigger is established.

## Deviation and edition check

The codified citations above are a baseline, not a substitute for the actual contract. The official [FAR-overhaul FAQ](https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/faqs) says rewritten parts are issued as model deviation text adopted by agencies. For an agency using that text, compare the actual deviation and contract clause, including renumbered make-or-buy provisions, before applying this matrix. DoD's [Part 15 deviation](https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/page_file_uploads/DoD_RFO_Deviation_Part-15.pdf) includes make-or-buy provisions and its [Part 44 deviation](https://www.acquisition.gov/sites/default/files/page_file_uploads/DoD_RFO_Deviation_Part-44.pdf) includes a deviation purchasing-system clause. Do not assert that codified and deviation text have identical thresholds, wording, or paragraph numbering. If exact applicability is unresolved, classify **Requires Review** and identify the contract/deviation text needed.

## Recommended analytical practice, not a regulation

Compare equal work scope and performance periods; document internal capability **and** capacity, fully loaded Buy costs, schedule dependencies, risk evidence, and non-lowest-cost reasoning. These checks support a useful decision record but are not represented as independently mandatory FAR checklist items.
