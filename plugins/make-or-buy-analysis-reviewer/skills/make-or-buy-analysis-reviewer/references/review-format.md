# Output formats

Choose the smallest format that completes the request. For a full review, use these headings and omit a heading only when plainly inapplicable (explain any material omission):

1. Requirement summary: scope, quantity or effort, deadline, contract context, and source status.
2. Alternatives evaluated: Make, Buy, and Hybrid if relevant; show material scope differences.
3. Cost comparison: common basis, line items, one-time and recurring amounts, total and arithmetic, exclusions, and evidence status. Label any percentage denominator and use the matching direction: `(Buy − Make) / Make = +5%` means **Buy is 5% higher than Make**, not “Make is 5% lower than Buy.”
4. Schedule and capacity assessment: required versus proposed dates, dependencies, capability versus available capacity.
5. Technical and operational risks: documented risks and mitigation, with subjective ratings attributed.
6. Small business considerations: only when relevant.
7. Regulatory and contractual considerations: distinguish routine comparison from incorporated formal program; identify exact clause or unresolved contract question.
8. Decision rationale assessment: preserve the user's selected approach and assess support, including non-lowest-cost rationale.
9. Findings and missing evidence: table with area, classification, evidence or gap, consequence, and next step. Use **Supported**, **Partially Supported**, **Unsupported**, or **Requires Review** exactly.
10. Recommended next steps: only material actions needed to resolve findings; assign to a role only when supported.

For an analysis under development, present a draft comparison and explicitly mark undecided fields. For a comparison-only request, a concise alternatives table plus material gaps and conclusion limits is enough. For formal-program-only review, present the applicability basis and contractor/Government actions without forcing a cost comparison.

When asked for a decision memorandum, produce a copy-ready memo with requirement, alternatives, comparison, selected approach, rationale, evidence references, and unresolved issues. Preserve the user's decision wording. Use targeted `[INSERT: specific missing fact]` placeholders when essential. Mark it **Draft — not an approval**. Do not fabricate signatories or approvals. Put review commentary and the validation reminder outside the memo.
