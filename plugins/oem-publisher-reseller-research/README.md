# OEM, Publisher & Reseller Research

Research public evidence of who makes, publishes, distributes, sells, supports, or renews a named product or service. The plugin maps direct sales, distributors, resellers, value-added resellers, integrators, marketplaces, and other relevant channels. It separates documented sellers from potential RFQ contacts and states when exact product, territory, customer-segment, or authorization scope is unverified.

## Use

Install the plugin from the [versioned ZIP](https://github.com/courtney-chapman/tools-public/releases/tag/oem-publisher-reseller-research-v0.1.5) or the [Tools Public marketplace](https://github.com/courtney-chapman/tools-public/blob/main/docs/install-plugins.md). Live public web research must be available to verify current claims.

Try:

- Map the documented direct, distributor, VAR, and integrator routes for this product, and identify potential RFQ contacts.
- Verify whether this reseller is authorized for this product and customer segment.
- Compare documented purchasing channels for this product.
- Check whether two part numbers identify the same product using the maker's mapping.

For each material finding, the plugin gives a source link and evidence classification, distinguishes supplier claims from maker verification, and flags open questions. A listing or broad partnership does not prove current authorization for a particular product or buyer. The plugin does not contact suppliers or determine adequate competition, price reasonableness, source justification, or supplier selection. Validate findings against current sources and your applicable contract and company requirements. Do not provide controlled or confidential records in a public research session.
