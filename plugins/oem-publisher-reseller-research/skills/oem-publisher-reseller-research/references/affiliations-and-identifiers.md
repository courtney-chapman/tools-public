# Affiliations and identifiers

For corporate relationships, check filings, official ownership disclosures, and acquisition or merger announcements. Distinguish parent, subsidiary, operating division, trade name, merger, and common control from partnership, common distributor, or shared product line. Similar names, addresses, sites, or catalogs do not establish common ownership. If ownership cannot be found, say it was not verified; do not declare the suppliers independent.

For product identity, compare the exact manufacturer part number, distributor or reseller SKU, product name, version, configuration, licensing tier, subscription term, deployment model, and replacement status. Separate an identical item from a compatible, aftermarket, refurbished, related, alternative configuration, different tier, successor, or rebranded item. Require manufacturer or publisher documentation for a confirmed cross-reference. A reseller mapping can be reported as a supplier claim. Similar descriptions or specifications alone do not establish technical or commercial equivalence.

If an identifier changes over time, record the source date and whether the mapping is current or historical. Explain which unresolved attribute prevents confirmation rather than guessing.
