# Authorization and channel policies

Verify authorization against an official manufacturer or publisher partner directory, authorized distributor listing, program documentation, announcement, or other controlling source when available. Check the precise authorization: sale, distribution, license, implementation, repair, maintenance, or support; then product line, territory, customer segment, tier, and effective period. Partner membership, consulting status, or service status alone does not establish rights to resell every offering. A reseller's own claim is `Supplier Claimed` until independently supported. Absence from a public directory is `Not Verified`, not proof of unauthorized status.

Keep these channel terms distinct:

- **Exclusive distributor:** official evidence grants exclusivity within a defined scope.
- **Sole listed distributor:** only one was found in reviewed sources; exclusivity is unproven.
- **Preferred distributor:** documented preference without proven exclusivity.
- **Designated distributor:** assigned to a stated product, market, or segment.
- **Authorized distributor:** officially recognized for a stated scope.
- **Master distributor:** documented supply to downstream partners.
- **Unverified arrangement:** the relationship or its nature cannot be established.

For exclusivity, look for official language and its product, territory, customer, and date limits. Do not infer it from promotional wording, a supplier's uncorroborated claim, or a limited search. If official and supplier sources conflict, report the conflict rather than selecting a favorable interpretation.

Research deal registration, opportunity protection, incentives, channel pricing structures, reseller switching, partner of record, renewal, license transfer, marketplace terms, warranty, support, maintenance, updates, entitlement, and gray-market or counterfeit warnings only when relevant. State a policy's documented purpose and public conditions. Do not assume it governs the buyer's transaction, a deal was registered, a partner receives preferential pricing, or a specific item is invalid, counterfeit, unsupported, or out of warranty. Never estimate confidential discounts, margins, or unpublished arrangements.
